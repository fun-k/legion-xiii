The Legion Oath of Service

Spoken after blessing by one of the Eight Divines

“I do solemnly swear to uphold the laws of the Empire. Before the Eight Divines and under the authority of the Ruby Throne, I bind my life and loyalty to the Empire of Tamriel and its Legion. I will obey the lawful commands of my officers, stand my post without fear, and fight the enemies of order, be they rebel, traitor, or invader. I swear to protect the citizens of the Empire, preserve peace and unity in its provinces, and serve with discipline, honor, and resolve, even unto death. By this oath, my life is given to the Legion, until my last breath, dismissed by those above me, or the end of the world. All hail the Empire, All hail the Emperor!”

Upon completion of this oath, the recruit is recognized as a Legionary of the Empire.
