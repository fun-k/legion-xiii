A House of Record

For twenty-five years the Imperial Province of Skyrim has stood without an active Imperial threat even, and over that time the Imperial Law has started to crack, be it from poor leadership, or influx of those who wish ill upon the Empire. That changes now.

The 13th Legion has been assigned to the Imperial Province of Skyrim under the careful eye of Governor Torg, and Legate Lucius Acacius. The Legion now stands as both a sign of peace, but also a warning of action. The Imperial Law will be enforced once more, and the Imperial Province of Skyrim’s chaos shall be crushed.

It was the Empire that brought unity to Tamriel
The Empire that crushed the Oblivion Crisis
And the Empire that endeavors for peace across all of Tamriel

Now the question is this? Where do you stand when all is settled? Will you become a forgotten story of what was? Or will you join the 13th Legion to become a protector of not only Skyrim, but all of Tamriel.

May those above judge me, and those below take me, if I fail in my duty.
